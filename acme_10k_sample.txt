PART I

ITEM 1. BUSINESS
Acme Robotics, Inc. ("Acme," "we," or "the Company") designs, manufactures, and
sells autonomous warehouse robots and the cloud software used to coordinate
them. We operate in two segments: Hardware and Cloud Services. The Company was
incorporated in Delaware in 2011 and is headquartered in Austin, Texas.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS
Our business is subject to numerous risks. A small number of large customers
account for a significant portion of revenue; the loss of any one of them could
materially harm our results. We depend on a limited number of component
suppliers, and disruptions in the semiconductor supply chain have in the past
delayed shipments. Our Cloud Services segment requires ongoing investment, and
we may not achieve profitability in that segment on the timeline we expect.

PART II

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION
Total revenue for fiscal 2024 was $1,284.6 million, an increase of 18.7% from
$1,082.3 million in fiscal 2023. The increase was driven primarily by growth in
Cloud Services revenue, which rose 41% year over year as customers expanded
their fleets. Hardware revenue grew 9% over the same period.

Gross profit was $565.2 million in fiscal 2024, representing a gross margin of
44.0%, up from 41.2% in fiscal 2023. The margin expansion reflected a favorable
shift in revenue mix toward higher-margin Cloud Services.

Operating income for fiscal 2024 was $142.0 million, compared to $98.7 million
in fiscal 2023. Net income was $108.4 million, or $1.92 per diluted share,
compared to net income of $74.1 million, or $1.33 per diluted share, in the
prior year.

We ended fiscal 2024 with cash and cash equivalents of $612.9 million and total
debt of $250.0 million. Free cash flow for fiscal 2024 was $96.3 million.

in fiscal 2024. Management expects Cloud Services to exceed 50% of total revenue
by fiscal 2026.
